# Assets Folder

This folder contains media assets for the Would You Rather video generator.

## Required Audio Files

Place the following audio files in the `audio/` directory:

### `audio/music.mp3`
- Background music for the video
- Recommended: Upbeat, energetic track
- Duration: Should loop or be longer than typical video (15+ seconds)
- Format: MP3, 192kbps or higher

### `audio/clock.mp3`
- Clock ticking sound
- Duration: ~3 seconds
- Plays during the countdown before revealing percentages
- Format: MP3

### `audio/ding.mp3`
- Bell or ding sound effect
- Short (~0.5-1 second)
- Plays when percentages are revealed
- Format: MP3

### `audio/swoosh.mp3`
- Swoosh/transition sound effect
- Short (~0.5-1 second)
- Plays at the end transition
- Format: MP3

## Required Image Files

Place the following image in the `images/` directory:

### `images/or.png`
- Background image for videos
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (vertical)
- Recommended resolution: 1080x1920px
- Should be visually appealing and work as a background
- Format: PNG or JPG

## Current Status

⚠️ **Audio files are currently not included in this repository.**

You need to add your own audio files to the `audio/` directory for full functionality. The video generator will work without them, but:
- No background music will play
- No clock ticking sound
- No ding sound
- No swoosh transition sound

The voice-over will still be generated by ElevenLabs API.
