Please place the following MP3 files in this directory:

1. **music.mp3** - Background music (looping, upbeat)
2. **clock.mp3** - Clock ticking sound (3 seconds)
3. **ding.mp3** - Ding/bell sound effect
4. **swoosh.mp3** - Swoosh transition sound
